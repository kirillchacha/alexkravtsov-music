Bio

Alex Kravtsov began playing piano at the age of six, drawn to the instrument long before he could reach the pedals. What started as a childhood curiosity soon became a lifelong devotion to composition and performance.

After years of formal training in classical piano and composition, Alex developed a voice that blends traditional orchestral structure with more intimate, contemporary textures — a balance he continues to explore in every new piece.

His work has been performed in concert halls and recorded for film and media projects alike, ranging from full orchestral scores to solo piano pieces written for quiet, reflective listening.

Today, Alex divides his time between composing new work, performing live, and collaborating with other musicians and directors — always looking for the next piece that hasn't been written yet.
